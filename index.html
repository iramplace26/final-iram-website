THE STORY OF IRAM

More Than Coffee

The name IRAM was inspired by the Quranic verse:

﴾ إِرَمَ ذَاتِ الْعِمَادِ ﴿

From this name, the idea was born to create a place that means more than just coffee — a space that brings together calmness, knowledge, work, and meaningful moments.

At IRAM, every corner is designed to offer a different experience; from open spaces for studying and working, to private rooms for meetings and teaching, and spaces where you can simply enjoy your time with friends.

PUBLIC AREA

Your Space, Your Way

An open space designed to fit your day. Study, work, read, or simply enjoy a cup of coffee in a comfortable and peaceful atmosphere.

Choose your spot, open your laptop, grab your coffee, and let the space help you accomplish more.

SILENT AREA

A Space for Quiet Focus

For those who prefer a quieter atmosphere, IRAM offers a dedicated Silent Area designed for studying, reading, working, and deep focus.

Put your phone aside, settle into your space, and enjoy a calm environment that helps you stay focused and productive.

PUBLIC & SILENT AREA

No entrance fee. Simply order your favorite drink and enjoy the space.

PRIVATE & TEACHING ROOMS

Spaces Designed for Focus

Whether you are looking for a quiet study room, a space for meetings, or a teaching room, IRAM offers a variety of spaces that can be booked to suit your needs.

Privacy, comfort, and the right environment to turn your time at IRAM into a more productive experience.

BOARD GAMES

Because Fun Is Part of the Experience

At IRAM, coffee and studying are not everything. We created a dedicated space for games where you can relax, enjoy time with your friends, and take a break from your daily routine.

Gather your friends, choose your game, and enjoy your time at IRAM.
